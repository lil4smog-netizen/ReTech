Asset Processing Operations

Stage A

Complete Media Declassification

We process every inbound storage device using software data sanitization loops. Drives that display read/write faults are physically crushed to ensure data protection.

Stage B

Workstation Integration

Functional processors and baseboards are chemically cleaned, re-paste treated, and integrated into sandbox server stacks for student programming labs.

Stage C

Material Reclamation

Fried nodes and shorted printed circuit boards are cleanly separated. Raw raw copper, gold contact pins, and alloy heat sinks are passed to eco-friendly recycling plants.
